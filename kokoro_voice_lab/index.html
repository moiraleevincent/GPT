What are we listening for? In the stress test, does “could” genuinely receive more contrastive emphasis? In the punctuation and pause tests, does the second half sound like a correction? In the pitch tests, does the voice merely get higher or lower—or does it convey a new intention?
Method notes and caveats

Original: speed 1.04, 35% Kore + 65% Nova. Stress: Kokoro markup on could and had. Punctuation: comma replaced by em dash. Directed pause: two separate synthesis calls, a 430-millisecond silence, and changed sentence boundaries. Faster: a fresh Kokoro synthesis at 1.08. Pitch: processed copies of the exact same original take, with ±4.5–5.5% shifts and tempo preservation where the FFmpeg filter allows. Distance: filtered highs and short echoes. The scene combines two voices and several controls.

These are listening experiments, not validated improvements. Pitch shifting the entire clip does not directly edit a word's intonation contour. Pauses and punctuation may change prosody in other ways too. The two-host scene is a new script rather than a same-text comparison.
