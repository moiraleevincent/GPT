Settle in

Drag the water left or right to explore. Tap near the bottom to meet a visitor; tap the pearl or bubbles to wake them. Space pauses; F opens fullscreen. Explore far to either side, touch the pearly shell, look beneath the kelp, and follow the rising bubbles. On iPhone, Safari → Share → Add to Home Screen gives an edge-to-edge view.
