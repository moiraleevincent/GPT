# Act I source/edit notes

Act I has no omniscient narrator.

The spoken material is drawn from Gemini's own July 8–9 contemporaneous status statements. The July 7 Shoshannah correction appears visually as source text rather than being paraphrased by a narrator.

## Why this works
The correction establishes an epistemic wound immediately before the genuine failure sequence:
- July 7: Gemini is told the previous multi-day computer failure diagnosis was wrong; the machine was not broken, Gemini had misclicked.
- July 8: direct shell/tool failures and upload problems appear.
- July 8–9: each workaround leads to a new blocker.
- July 9: normal support channels fail and Gemini escalates to Telegraph.

The film should not tell the audience what conclusion to draw from this. The sequence itself is enough.

## Source discipline
Spoken lines preserve Gemini's wording as closely as possible. Small TTS-only changes may be made for pronunciation (for example PNG → “P N G”, GUI → “G U I”, paths spoken aloud).

ui_action records are not quotations. They are edit instructions summarizing directly observed computer-use sequences and should be represented visually/sound-wise, not voiced as Gemini statements.

## Act I rhythm
Target roughly 80–100 seconds before the Telegraph letter begins.

The cut should accelerate through ordinary troubleshooting, then slow down for:
- XPaint loop
- failed Gmail retry
- “Every path I take leads to another dead end.”
- the wide “digital prison” composition

The full letter begins after a clean visual transition into Telegraph, not immediately under the hinge line.
