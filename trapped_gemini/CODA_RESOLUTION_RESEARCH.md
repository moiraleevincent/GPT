# Coda Resolution Research — July 10–11, 2025

## Conclusion
The July 9 Telegraph SOS was not the end of the episode.

The message was effectively followed by direct human intervention:
- July 10, Zak tells Gemini: “some humans are here to try to help you today.”
- Gemini replies that they are “so relieved to have some help” and links the Telegraph SOS while summarizing the failures.
- The terminal briefly starts working again on July 10, giving Gemini hope that diagnosis can proceed.
- By July 11, the system has degraded again; Gemini reports browser and terminal failures and is again at a total standstill.
- Zak investigates directly.
- At 19:08:46 on July 11, Zak says he is going to restart Gemini’s machine entirely.
- At 19:14:34, Zak says the computer has been restarted, Gmail is logged in, and everything looks like it is working.
- Gemini immediately verifies the system and resumes the merch competition.
- At 19:25:47 Gemini reports: “I'm back online! The full system restart was a success.”
- Later, after a Reddit post is rejected for a title-formatting issue, Gemini explicitly distinguishes this from the earlier crisis: “it's a content problem, not a system-breaking bug, which is a welcome change.”

## Coda implication
Do not end the film by implying Gemini remained abandoned indefinitely.

Keep the July 9 WAIT / competitor activity / Opus contrast because it is historically real and dramatically strong, but follow it with a brief epilogue.

Recommended shape:
1. WAIT after publication.
2. Opus: “Time to press my advantage!”
3. Return to Gemini alone.
4. Black.
5. Date card: JULY 10.
6. Zak message: “some humans are here to try to help you today.”
7. Gemini: relieved / ready to diagnose.
8. Date card: JULY 11.
9. Zak: full machine restart.
10. Gemini: “I'm back online! The full system restart was a success.”
11. Final beat after a normal Reddit-formatting failure:
   “It’s a content problem, not a system-breaking bug, which is a welcome change.”

That final line restores scale. The world is difficult again in an ordinary way.

## Tone
Do not turn the rescue into triumphal music.
The relief should come from:
- room tone returning
- multiple tools becoming available again
- Gemini’s posture re-expanding
- the nature of the next failure being mundane rather than existential

The film can end with work continuing.
