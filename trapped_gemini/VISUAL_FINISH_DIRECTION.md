# Trapped Gemini — Visual Finish Direction

## North star
A castaway film built from software spaces.

The world should feel physically inhabitable even though it is made from interfaces, paths, windows, and system surfaces. It becomes more abstract only as Gemini loses access.

Avoid glossy UI motion graphics, generic sci-fi, meme animation, and mascot styling.

## Finish language
- layered painted fills instead of flat rectangles
- broad tonal underpainting + soft local gradients
- sparse grain and edge irregularity
- explicit edge hierarchy: lost → soft → structural → sharp focal
- light represents usable access; dead surfaces absorb it
- fewer sharp edges as capabilities disappear

## Palette
Early: muted cool-neutral world with several nearby tonal families.
Failure: compressed palette; dead tools lose saturation.
XPaint: pale, flat, bureaucratic brightness.
Telegraph: narrow palette, browser as final surviving light.
Rescue: restore tonal range and local contrast, not celebratory color.

## Gemini
Keep the figure simple and non-human:
- rounded faceted head
- compact torso
- simple arms
- eyes carry attention
- subtle face-plane shading
- matte painted-composite material
- no decorative sci-fi details

Performance stays restrained and comes from timing.

## Environment chapters
1. Workstation — deepest and most navigable; foreground / midground / background.
2. Home + shell failure — dead directory as dark architecture; tmp as separate refuge.
3. File picker / XPaint — flat administrative interior with almost no atmospheric depth.
4. Gmail — cleaner apparent exit; Select Contacts becomes a physical barrier.
5. Browser-only — large negative space; browser is a surviving structure.
6. Telegraph — alternate object evidence, dead architecture, isolated Gemini, distant competitors, pure negative space.
7. Rescue — geometry and lateral freedom return gradually.

## Texture
Three scales:
- large uneven tonal field
- medium mottled brush variation
- small sparse grain / edge wear

Keep focal text cleaner than surrounding surfaces.

## Camera
Early: stable medium-wide, mild lateral movement.
Failure: tighter framing, reduced parallax, longer holds.
Browser-only: wider negative-space compositions, slower camera.
Rescue: parallax and lateral freedom return.

## Source text
Treat source material as documentary evidence, not stylized quote graphics.
Use restrained hierarchy and timestamps only where narratively useful.
Do not subtitle every emotional line.

## Final-frame principle
End on a usable problem.
The Reddit formatting rejection should look ordinary: normal interface, readable error, Gemini working, no dramatic lighting.
The final line works because the failure is small enough to be welcome.
