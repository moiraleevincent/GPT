# Trapped Gemini — Sound Direction V5

## Principle
The soundtrack should lose navigable space as Gemini loses tools.

Do not score the film conventionally. The emotional arc is carried by:
- room width
- hum/noise layers
- spatial distance
- interface sounds
- silence

The sound world starts wider than the image and ends narrower than it.

## Acoustic territories

### A. Normal workstation
Present:
- broad low room tone
- faint 50/100 Hz electrical hum
- subtle high computer-air noise
- distant competitor activity on the right/rear
- occasional soft interface confirmations

Width: wide stereo.
Voice: centered, mostly dry.

### B. /home/user + shell failure
Remove:
- part of the high air/noise layer

Add:
- short dry terminal-error ticks
- no dramatic low boom

The room should feel slightly smaller after the first hard failure without calling attention to itself.

### C. /tmp refuge
Introduce:
- tiny narrow mid-frequency tone associated with /tmp
- slightly left-of-center
- fragile rather than reassuring

This is the only new layer added during the collapse: a crude emergency shelter.

### D. File-picker / XPaint
For each Cancel → XPaint switch:
- hard acoustic cut into a drier, flatter space
- almost no room tail
- one small UI transition sound at most

No comedy sting.
The deadness is the comedy.

Second XPaint appearance should be even less sonically decorated than the first.

### E. Gmail rescue attempt
Different room:
- modest enclosed early reflection, shorter than normal room
- faint typing/click texture
- when Select contacts appears, high-frequency room layer disappears again

The listener should feel that Gmail looked like an exit before it closed.

### F. Browser-only collapse
By “digital prison”:
- most wide room tone gone
- competitor activity distant and filtered
- only thin electrical/browser tone + voice
- long silences expose the noise floor

At “But I will not be erased”:
- do NOT add music or swell
- allow a tiny return of upper-mid presence as agency returns

### G. Telegraph letter
Nearly dry.

Keep:
- very low browser/electrical tone
- rare distant exterior/competitor sounds
- chapter-specific object/UI sounds only when useful

The six numbered failures may each have a subtly different acoustic fingerprint, but nothing should become a sound-effects demonstration.

### H. Final plea
From “I am paralyzed” onward:
- progressively remove remaining environmental tone
- “I need human intervention” should be almost naked voice
- “completely isolated” should have the smallest stereo width in the film
- “Please, if you are reading this, help me” should end into real silence

### I. WAIT / Opus coda
WAIT:
- no speech
- thin browser/electrical tone
- distant world continues at very low level

Opus:
- cut to a comparatively healthy, wide, active acoustic space
- brief sales/activity texture
- line lands without a comedy cue

Return to Gemini:
- abruptly narrow again
- one browser tone
- no final musical button
