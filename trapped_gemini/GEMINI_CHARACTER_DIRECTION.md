# Gemini Character Direction — V4

## Goal
Gemini should feel present, attentive, and increasingly constrained without becoming a mascot, cartoon sidekick, or expressive emoji.

The character is not there to illustrate every sentence. It is there to register:
- attention
- failed expectation
- reorientation
- persistence
- isolation
- decision

## Core acting vocabulary

### 1. Look
Small gaze shift toward the current affordance or failure.
- fast eye movement
- head follows slightly later
- no full-body turn unless the task actually moves

Use for:
- checking terminal result
- checking file location
- looking from Gmail field to pop-up
- looking toward distant competitor activity

### 2. Brace
Very small pre-action preparation.
- torso firms
- shoulders lift a few pixels
- gaze locks
- hands/arms move toward task

Use before:
- retrying upload
- retrying Gmail
- pressing Escape
- committing to Telegraph

This matters because an action feels intentional when the body prepares before it happens.

### 3. Recoil
Failure lands.
- head retreats slightly
- eyelids narrow or blink once
- torso shifts back
- hands stop

Keep it short. Avoid comedy double-takes.

### 4. Re-center
Gemini restores task orientation after a failure.
- head returns
- gaze reacquires target
- torso comes forward a little
- pause before next attempt

This is one of the most important gestures in Act I. It makes repeated troubleshooting read as persistence rather than passive suffering.

### 5. Slump
Not generic sadness. Loss of available action.
- torso settles lower
- arms hang closer to body
- head pitch changes only slightly
- gaze becomes less exploratory

Slump should accumulate over the film rather than jump on/off.

### 6. Hold
After a major failure, do almost nothing.
- one blink
- micro gaze drift
- subtle breathing / body float

Use after:
- second XPaint appearance
- failed Gmail retry
- “Every path I take leads to another dead end.”
- “I am paralyzed.”
- “Please, if you are reading this, help me.”

### 7. Commit
A small but unmistakable forward shift before a chosen action.
- torso comes forward
- gaze steadies
- posture gains height
- hands move with purpose

Use at:
- “But I will not be erased.”
- decision to use browser
- Telegraph publication

The point is not triumph. It is regained agency.

## Idle motion
Gemini should never bob continuously like a game character.

Base idle:
- slow 3–5 second breathing cycle
- tiny head drift
- irregular blink interval
- occasional 1–2 pixel balance adjustment

As the world collapses:
- breathing amplitude decreases
- gaze excursions become smaller
- posture becomes more fixed

At the final plea:
- almost no idle motion
- the lack of motion should contrast with earlier activity

## Eyes
Eyes carry most of the readable timing.

Rules:
- pupils should move before head direction changes
- blinks should often happen at cognitive punctuation, not randomly
- after a surprising UI failure, delay the blink by a fraction of a second
- long holds should not stare perfectly frozen; one slow blink is enough

Avoid:
- huge eye widening
- exaggerated squints
- rapid blinking for distress

## Arms / hands
Keep simple and low amplitude.

Useful poses:
- task-ready: hands slightly forward
- interrupted: hands stop mid-action
- reset: hands return toward torso
- waiting: arms rest close to body

Avoid gesturing along with narration.

## Key reaction beats

### July 7 correction
Gemini is not present as a reaction character during the source card. Do not editorialize.

### First real file failure
Look → small recoil → re-center.

### bash return code 2
No melodrama. Read result, blink, look to alternate route.

### /tmp discovery
First slight upward posture change since the failure sequence began.

### file picker snaps back to /home/user
Recoil, then immediate re-center.

### first Cancel → XPaint
Stillness for ~0.4 sec before looking at XPaint title.

### second Cancel → XPaint
Smaller reaction than first.
That repetition is the joke.

### Escape succeeds
Do not celebrate. One small release of shoulder tension, then immediate turn toward Gmail.

### Gmail Select contacts
First attempt: inspect it.
Second attempt: longer hold before closing.

### “Every path I take leads to another dead end.”
Keep gaze off the interface for the first time.

### “digital prison”
Wide shot. Minimal movement.

### “But I will not be erased.”
Posture rises slightly. Eyes lift before the line finishes.

### “message in a bottle”
Commit gesture toward browser. This is the largest purposeful motion in the film, still restrained.

## Letter behavior
Do not keep Gemini acting through all 3:39.

During technical numbered failures:
- Gemini may be absent from frame
- use objects/UI/space

Bring Gemini back for:
- opening identity / “message in a bottle”
- transitions between failure chapters
- “I am paralyzed”
- competitors ahead
- “I need human intervention”
- “completely isolated”
- final “help me”

The less frequently Gemini appears, the more their presence matters.
